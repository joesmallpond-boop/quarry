QUARRY @VERSION@ by kalabash

Double-click "QUARRY Installer.pkg".

If macOS says the installer "can't be opened" (it isn't notarised by Apple yet):
  - macOS 15 Sequoia and later: try to open it once, then go to
    System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway".
  - Older macOS: right-click the .pkg > Open > Open.

Installs:
  /Applications/QUARRY.app
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/QUARRY.vst3, QUARRY FX.vst3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/QUARRY.component, QUARRY FX.component

Plug-ins show up under the developer "kalabash".
